## ⚡ Super Quick Overview

**What happens in setup:**

1. **Create Firebase project** (free!)
2. **Copy your Firebase config** to `public/js/config.js`
3. **Download service account key** to `config/firebase-credentials.json`
4. **Run migration script** to import your Excel data
5. **Push to GitHub** and enable Pages
6. **Share URL** with your team!

**Result:**
- Live web app at: `https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/ymu-gala-2026/`
- Real-time data sync for 2-5 team members
